# Experience

## Freelance Web Developer at goWebgo (January 2023 to present)
Athens and remote. Websites, e-shops, internal tools, hosting and IT for small businesses. goWebgo is his own web studio; the design partner is saavet designs, who handles design, branding, UI, social media and content.

What he did:
- Connected nine client sites to Claude Code over MCP with Novamira. The agent works on the site's PHP, database and files directly, so a client request turns into a tested change quickly.
- Built internal apps inside client sites: a members area where each parent sees only their own children, admin panels run from a phone, an inquiry flow for three venues, and an auto-generated class schedule.
- Delivered 20 websites and e-shops end to end, from a client's brief to launch and maintenance, iterating with the client until it does what they need.
- Wrote custom WordPress themes and plugins in PHP; WooCommerce stores with Viva.com card payments, stock, search and online ordering.
- Implemented Figma designs with Saavet Designs, with responsive layouts tested on mobile.
- Managed deployment and hosting: Cloudflare, DNS, SSL, business email and migrations between providers.

## goWebgo services
- Websites and e-shops: fast, mobile-ready, delivered with a client admin panel.
- Applications: member systems, bookings, match centers and internal systems, when a website is not enough.
- Hosting and maintenance with backups and updates.
- Business email on the client's domain, and newsletters.
- IT support for small companies: computers, networks and accounts.
- Every client gets an admin panel built around their work, so they can edit texts, prices, photos, schedules and products themselves, also from a phone.

## goWebgo process
1. Introduction and goals: a brief on the business, its audience and what the site must achieve.
2. Design: saavet designs makes wireframes and mockups, with feedback rounds before development.
3. Development: a fast, mobile-ready build with the admin panel included from the start.
4. Testing: on mobile devices and browsers, speed measurement and Google readiness.
5. Launch and support: going live, a walkthrough of the admin panel, backups and ongoing support.

A 5 to 8 page company site usually takes 3 to 5 weeks once texts and photos are ready.
